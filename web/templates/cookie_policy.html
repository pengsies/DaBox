1. Scope and acceptance

This policy applies to all cookies and comparable technologies (such as local storage, pixel tags, and similar identifiers, to the extent we use them) set by RelayForge when you access the Service through a supported web browser. It does not apply to any third-party website you may reach through a relay connection you request, which is governed by that destination's own policies, nor does it apply to the native Worker tunnel protocol, which does not use cookies at all. By continuing to use the Service after this policy takes effect, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review it.

2. What are cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. Cookies are widely used to make websites function, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information and support personalization. Cookies set by the website you are visiting are usually referred to as "first-party cookies," and typically only relate to information and preferences generated on that site. We do not currently set any third-party cookies; see Section 10 for details on how that would change if it ever does.

Depending on their duration, cookies are either "session cookies," which are deleted when you close your browser, or "persistent cookies," which remain on your device for a defined period or until you delete them manually. Depending on their purpose, cookies may be classified as strictly necessary, functional, performance/analytics, or targeting/ advertising cookies. RelayForge currently uses only the first two categories, described in the table in Section 7.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or a similar framework applies to our processing of your personal data through cookies, we rely on the following legal bases, as applicable:

  • Performance of a contract. Strictly necessary cookies are required to deliver the Service you have requested by creating an account and signing in.
  • Legitimate interests. To the extent any cookie supports the security, reliability, or basic operability of the Service, we rely on our legitimate interest in operating a functioning platform, balanced against your rights and freedoms.
  • Consent. Where a cookie is not strictly necessary, we rely on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this domain. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

4. Your rights under applicable data protection law

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have some or all of the following rights in relation to personal data processed through cookies: the right to be informed about our processing; the right of access to the personal data we hold about you; the right to rectification of inaccurate data; the right to erasure in certain circumstances; the right to restrict processing; the right to data portability; the right to object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests; and rights related to automated decision-making and profiling, none of which we currently perform on the basis of cookie data alone. These rights are not absolute and may be subject to conditions or exceptions under applicable law.

5. Consent, preference management, and withdrawal

Where our use of cookies depends on consent, you may grant, withhold, or later withdraw that consent at any time, and withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Withdrawing consent normally requires clearing or blocking the relevant cookie through your browser settings; where the Service offers an in-product control, that control will be presented before any non-essential cookie is set. Because RelayForge currently relies only on strictly necessary and functional cookies, no separate consent gate is displayed, and continued use of the Service constitutes acknowledgement of the cookies described in Section 7. Disabling functional cookies may cause certain preferences to reset on your next visit, while disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent you from signing in at all.

6. Cookie categories and classification methodology

For the purposes of this policy, and consistent with common supervisory guidance, cookies are classified according to the function they perform rather than the technology used to implement them. A cookie may fall into more than one category where its purpose overlaps, and the category assigned reflects the primary purpose for which the cookie was introduced. The categories are: (a) strictly necessary, which are essential to route requests, maintain security, and provide a service you have requested; (b) functional, which remember choices you make in order to improve your experience; (c) performance and analytics, which help us understand how the Service is used; and (d) targeting and advertising, which are used to deliver content relevant to you. We review and re-classify cookies as the Service evolves, and the table in Section 7 reflects the position as at the date shown at the top of this page.

8. International data transfers and safeguards

Cookie data is processed on infrastructure operated for RelayForge and is not knowingly transferred outside the environment in which the Service is deployed. If a future deployment involves cross-border processing, we will implement appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism recognized under applicable law, before any such transfer occurs, and we will update this policy to identify the relevant transfer mechanism and the categories of recipient involved.

9. Data retention and storage limitation

We retain cookie data only for as long as the cookie itself remains valid, as described in Section 7. We do not maintain a separate server-side log of historical cookie values beyond what is required to process your current request. Session data is discarded on logout or expiry; functional preference data is discarded when its cookie expires or is cleared. Where the same information is held for more than one purpose, we retain it only for as long as the longest applicable retention period requires, and then delete or irreversibly anonymise it.

10. Third-party cookies, analytics, and service providers

RelayForge does not currently embed third-party analytics, advertising, or social media widgets, and therefore does not currently set or permit any third-party cookies on the Service. Should we introduce any such integration in the future, this section will be updated to name the provider, describe the categories of data shared, set out the relevant retention periods, and, where required, a consent mechanism will be presented before any non-essential third-party cookie is set. Where a service provider processes cookie-derived data on our behalf, it does so only under a written contract that limits its processing to our documented instructions.

11. Cookie lifecycle, technical storage, and internal preference objects

Cookies may be implemented as session cookies, which are held in memory and discarded when the browsing session ends, or as persistent cookies, which are written to your device for a defined period or until you delete them. Functional preference data is stored as an opaque, encoded value associated with your browser rather than with any third party, and the Service does not attempt to correlate it with your activity on other sites. The table in Section 7 lists the cookies by name and purpose; the following note describes the internal representation of one functional cookie, because it is the cookie most likely to be examined in a privacy or security review.

12. Security measures and confidentiality

All cookies are transmitted exclusively over TLS-encrypted connections. Cookies that do not need to be read by client-side scripts are marked HttpOnly to reduce exposure to cross-site scripting. Session and functional cookies are scoped with a restrictive SameSite attribute to reduce exposure to cross-site request forgery. Access to the systems that issue and validate these cookies is limited to the services that require it as a matter of internal design, and we review those access boundaries periodically. No method of transmission or storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information held in cookies.

13. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control, and opt-out signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal, and some also support the Global Privacy Control (GPC). There is currently no universally accepted standard governing how DNT signals must be interpreted, and RelayForge does not alter its use of strictly necessary or functional cookies in response to DNT. Where applicable law requires us to honour GPC or a comparable opt-out preference signal, we will treat such a signal as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; because RelayForge does not sell personal information and does not currently set advertising cookies, no opt-out from sale or sharing is required.

14. Automated decision-making and profiling

RelayForge does not use cookies to make solely automated decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you, and does not build behavioural profiles for advertising purposes. Information derived from strictly necessary cookies may be used to detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access, and that processing is carried out on the basis of our legitimate interests in protecting the Service and its users. Should we ever introduce automated decision-making that engages your rights, we will update this policy and provide a means to request human intervention.

15. Children's privacy and age assurance

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data, including through cookies, from anyone in that age group. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the details in Section 22 so we can take appropriate action, which may include deleting the relevant information from our systems.

23. Definitions and interpretation

  • Cookie means a small text file placed on your device by a website and read back by that website on later visits.
  • Session cookie means a cookie that is discarded when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookie means a cookie that remains on your device for a defined period or until you delete it.
  • First-party cookie means a cookie set by the website you are visiting; a third-party cookie is one set by a party other than that website.
  • Personal data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Processing means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, and disclosure.
  • Consent means a freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of your wishes.
  • Supervisory authority means an independent public authority responsible for monitoring the application of data protection law in a given jurisdiction.
  • Service means the RelayForge access portal described at the top of this policy.
